About Me

The story behind Through My Frames

📷 Photography
| 💻 Web Development
| 🌿 Storytelling

Hi, I'm Ayush Mishra, a first-year Mechanical Engineering student with a deep passion for photography and web development. I believe every photograph holds a story, and through my camera, I love capturing moments that deserve to be remembered.
Photography has taught me to notice the beauty in simple things—whether it's a quiet morning, a rainy day, a village road, or the colors of nature. Every picture I take is a memory that reflects how I see the world.
Alongside photography, I enjoy learning web development. I love turning ideas into websitesand continuously improving myskills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Building projects allows me to combine creativity with technology, and every new feature I create teaches me something valuable.
Through My Frames is more than just a photography portfolio. It is a collection of my memories,my learning journey,and my growth as both a photographer and a developer. Thank you for taking the time to visit my website—I hope you enjoy exploring it as much as I enjoyed creating it.
"Every frame tells a story, and this is mine."

— Ayush Mishra
