The story behind

Through My Frames

📷 Photography 💻 Web Development 🌿 Storytelling

Hi, I'm Ayush Mishra, a first-year Mechanical Engineering student with a deep passion for photography and web development.

I believe every photograph holds a story. Through my camera, I love capturing moments that deserve to be remembered.

Photography has taught me to notice the beauty in simple things— a quiet morning, a rainy day, a village road or the colours of nature. Every picture I take is a memory that reflects how I see the world.

Alongside photography, I enjoy learning web development. I love turning ideas into websites and continuously improving my skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Through My Frames is more than a photography portfolio. It is a collection of my memories, my learning journey and my growth as both a photographer and a developer.

“Every frame tells a story, and this is mine.” — Ayush Mishra
