📍 Agra • India

Some places do not feel like destinations. They feel like pauses.

Story

The Taj Mahal is counted among the Seven Wonders of the World.And honestly, the moment I saw it for the first time with my own eyes, I understood why it deserves that place. 🫡
This photo was taken during my first college trip with my friends. The Taj Mahal tells a story of history, architecture, and India's rich cultural heritage.It was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan. In the memory his beloved queen Mumtaz Mahal.
And really when you visit it (if you look beyond religion)it truly feels like a symbol of love....Standing in front of the Taj Mahal, everything felt strangely quiet. People were moving, cameras were clicking, but for a moment, it felt like time had decided to slow down.

Reflection

We spend so much time trying to capture moments. But sometimes, a moment captures us instead.

Some places are remembered. Some places stay with you.
